Our journey

A Proud History of Growth

St. Thomas Kavingoni School was established in 1983 with the vision of providing quality, holistic and affordable education to learners from Kavingoni and surrounding communities. What began as a modest institution with a small enrolment has grown into a respected centre of learning known for academic excellence, discipline, integrity and character development.

Over the years, the school has grown through the commitment of dedicated teachers, supportive parents, the Board of Management, alumni, sponsors and the wider community. Their collective contribution has strengthened infrastructure, academic programmes and the learning environment.

Today, our educational experience combines academic learning with leadership development, spiritual growth, sports, co-curricular activities, environmental conservation, innovation and digital literacy. Learners are encouraged to become confident, responsible and compassionate citizens prepared for the modern world.

Guided by our motto, “Education For A Better Life,” we continue to nurture learners who can positively impact their communities, Kenya and the world.
